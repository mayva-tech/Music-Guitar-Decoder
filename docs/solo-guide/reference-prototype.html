Land on chord tones

The trick that makes a solo fit

Every scale note is safe, but the notes of the chord that's playing right now sound strongest, especially on beat 1. Pick a chord to see its notes ringed inside the box, then compare the two versions.

    Try this
    
        
    • Use only three notes for a whole chorus. Make them interesting with rhythm.
    • Play two bars, then rest for two. Answer your own phrase.
    • Land on a ringed chord tone every time the chord changes.
    • One bend per bar, no more.
    • Leave a whole bar empty. Then come back higher.

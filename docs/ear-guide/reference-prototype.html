Music Decoder · ear guide

Play any song by ear

A seven-step method for working out a song from listening alone. Find home, decide major or minor, follow the bass, fill in the chords, then the melody and rhythm. Every step has examples you can hear, two songs are worked out from start to finish, and four drills train your ear.

The method

Seven steps, in this order

The order matters. Each step narrows the next one down, so by the time you reach the chords you only have a handful to try.

Worked examples

Two songs, start to finish

Both are traditional tunes you probably already know. Play each stage, then try the same steps on a song you like.

Ear drills

Train the skills each step needs

Each question is random. Listen as many times as you like before answering. A few minutes a day is enough to hear a difference within a couple of weeks.
